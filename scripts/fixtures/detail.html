Open Rec Skate

Open Rec Skate

local_offer Loading Price...
draw Waivers are required.
NO REGISTRATION REQUIRED - Open Recreation Skate is designed to maximize participation, and to be able to accommodate all types of users. Come with a group of friends, or by yourself, to meet new people. Open Recreation Skate will have a consistent time throughout the semester.

**Please Note**
- All users must have a valid Warrior Recreation membership to utilize the Open Recreation space.
- Proper skate attire is required, including skates and a CSA approved helmet if needed
- A skate monitor is on site to support users and will make the final decision on requirements of helmets.
- Skates are not provided. There are rental opportunities in Waterloo through a number of facilities

Furthermore, note that days and times are subject to change. Participants should check each schedule before coming to skate.
